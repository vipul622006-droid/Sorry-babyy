💌 Dil Ki Baat

Meri Jaan, Kabhi kabhi insaan apni sabse important cheez ki value tab samajhta hai jab woh usse dukh pahucha deta hai. Main jaanta hoon ki maine tumhe hurt kiya. Aur ye baat mujhe har din andar se feel hoti hai. Tum meri zindagi ka woh hissa ho jise main kabhi khona nahi chahta. Main tumhe sirf pyaar nahi karta... Main tumhari respect karta hoon, tumhari care karta hoon, aur tumhare saath apna poora future dekhta hoon. Aaj main sirf ek mauka maang raha hoon. Ek mauka khud ko better prove karne ka. Ek mauka tumhe phir se smile karwane ka. Ek mauka tumhara trust jeetne ka. ❤️
