💔 Meri Galti

Meri Jaan, Aaj main tumse koi bahana nahi banaunga. Main ye nahi kahunga ki situation aisi thi... ya meri majboori thi... Sach sirf itna hai ki maine tumhe dukh diya. Aur ye baat mujhe andar se tod deti hai. Jab se tum naraz ho, tab se har din adhura lagta hai. Tumhari khamoshi bhi mujhe hazaar sawalon se zyada chubhti hai. Main jaanta hoon ki trust aur feelings bahut precious hoti hain. Aur agar maine unhe hurt kiya hai, to uska regret mujhe har pal hota hai. Main perfect nahi hoon. Mujhse galti hui hai. Bahut badi galti. Lekin meri mohabbat tumhare liye kabhi kam nahi hui. Aaj main sirf itna kehna chahta hoon... Sorry. Dil se sorry. Sach me sorry. ❤️
